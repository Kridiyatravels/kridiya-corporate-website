Accommodation

Stay close to the work.

Hotels for business trips, project teams and longer stays. Compare the location, room type and inclusions against your company’s budget.

What to include in your request

  • City and preferred work location
  • Check-in and check-out dates
  • Rooms, occupants and meal plan
  • Budget and cancellation needs

Tell us where the team needs to be each day, not only the city. That helps us compare practical locations as well as room prices.

What happens next

  1. Compare stays

    Compare room types, inclusions and cancellation terms.

  2. Choose your room

    Choose the property, room type and dates from the quoted options.

  3. Check the voucher

    Receive a booking voucher after supplier confirmation.

Approval and confirmation

Room prices depend on dates, occupancy and availability. Review the quoted meal plan and cancellation terms before paying. Bookings are prepaid; an LPO does not provide credit.
