How we use your information.

Information collected through our enquiry forms and company workspace, how we use it, and how to contact us.

Who handles the information

Kridiya Travel and Tourism FZ-LLC handles the information described on this page for its corporate travel enquiry and workspace services.

Information you provide

Public forms collect the contact, company and travel requirements you submit. The private workspace can hold company membership, traveller, request, quote, booking, document, payment-evidence and support records used to manage company travel.

Provide only the details needed for the request. Public enquiry forms are not intended for passport copies, passport numbers or payment-card information.

How the information is used

Kridiya uses the information to respond to enquiries, review company applications, manage permitted account access, prepare travel options, record decisions and coordinate requested services. Booking fulfilment may require relevant information to be provided to travel suppliers, subject to the service being arranged.

Services used by this website

Enquiry records and authenticated workspace services use Supabase. After a public form is saved, FormSubmit sends our desk its reference; the travel and billing details stay in the enquiry record. Website fonts are requested from Google Fonts, and the Supabase browser library is loaded from jsDelivr when needed.

Opening a WhatsApp link takes you to WhatsApp. These providers process connection or submitted information as part of their services. Contact the desk if you need clarification before submitting.

Processing outside the UAE

Travel arrangements may require relevant information to be handled in the country of departure, transit or destination by airlines, hotels, ticketing partners, visa providers or other suppliers involved in the requested service. Website and workspace providers may also process information in other countries. Contact the corporate desk if you need details about a particular service before submitting information.

Account storage and access

The authenticated workspace stores session information in your browser to keep you signed in. Use your own login, sign out on shared devices and keep your password private. Company records are available according to approved company membership and permissions.

This website does not add advertising trackers through its corporate scripts. Browser storage used for account sessions and display preferences serves the workspace functionality.

How long information is kept

Information is kept while it is needed to review an enquiry, manage the company workspace, provide an arranged service, maintain required booking or financial records, resolve a dispute, or meet a legal obligation. The period varies by record and service. Closing an account does not remove records that must still be kept for one of these purposes.

Your requests

Subject to applicable law, you may ask for access to your information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing. Verification may be required before company or traveller information is discussed or changed.

Contact contact@kridiyatravel.com about a privacy request. Include enough context to locate the relevant company or request, but do not email passwords, full payment-card details or unnecessary identity documents.
