How it works

From travel request to confirmed booking.

Your team reviews the quote and approves the cost. Kridiya checks payment, arranges the booking and provides the travel documents.

1. Apply for a company account

Send your company details, travel needs, billing contact and approval requirements. We’ll review the application and arrange access for your team.

2. Send your travel requirements

Add the route, dates, number of travellers, budget and any deadlines. Existing clients can use the company workspace; new clients can use the travel request form.

3. Review the options

The desk checks the requirement and prepares a quote. Review inclusions, validity, baggage, change conditions and exclusions. Ask for a revision before approving if anything is unclear.

4. Approve the quote and arrange payment

Your company’s approver accepts the quote. Bookings are prepaid: the desk confirms receipt of payment before arranging the service, subject to availability. A purchase order does not replace payment. Wait for booking confirmation before treating the trip as booked.

5. Receive confirmation and documents

Check the booking confirmation, tickets and vouchers. Let the desk know promptly if a name, date or itinerary detail needs correcting.

6. Request changes and review records

Quote the booking reference when requesting a change. Your company’s finance contact can review receipts and monthly activity reports and ask the desk about missing or incorrect records.
