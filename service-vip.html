Executive travel

Travel planned around a demanding schedule.

Coordinate executive itineraries with an authorised arranger, preferred timings and clearly agreed travel preferences.

What to include in your request

  • Executive or authorised arranger contact
  • Route and schedule constraints
  • Cabin and accommodation preferences
  • Privacy and assistance requirements

Tell us which timings are fixed and where there is flexibility. Include the arranger’s contact details so questions reach the right person.

What happens next

  1. Set priorities

    Agree the traveller’s priorities and who will review the options.

  2. Compare options

    Review premium options and change terms.

  3. Keep the itinerary current

    Check the confirmed itinerary and send any change requests through the nominated contact.

Approval and confirmation

Premium cabins, preferred rooms and assistance services are subject to availability. Review the quoted change conditions before approval. Bookings are prepaid; an LPO does not provide credit.
