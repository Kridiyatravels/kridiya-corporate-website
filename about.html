Company travel, personally handled

Kridiya Business Travel is the corporate travel service of Kridiya Travel and Tourism FZ-LLC. We coordinate company travel requirements across flights, stays, visas, transfers, groups and related support.

We start with your destinations, travel frequency, budget and approval requirements. These details help the desk prepare relevant options for your team.

A working relationship with your team

The travel desk checks options, prepares quotes and follows up on the details needed to arrange a booking. Your company workspace lets your team review quotes, follow bookings and collect documents.

If your procurement team needs company or licensing documents, contact the corporate desk.
