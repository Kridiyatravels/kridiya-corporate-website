Meetings & events

Travel that fits the event schedule.

Flights, hotels and transfers for meetings, incentives, conferences and exhibitions, planned around the event organiser’s schedule.

What to include in your request

  • Venue and programme dates
  • Delegate departure cities and numbers
  • Accommodation and meeting requirements
  • Arrival windows and transfer schedule

Share the event programme or key timings. We can then plan arrivals, departures and transfers around the sessions people need to attend.

What happens next

  1. Map the schedule

    Agree the travel requirements and who is handling the venue and event programme.

  2. Review travel options

    Review options and supplier deadlines.

  3. Share confirmed arrangements

    Share confirmed itineraries and travel documents with the nominated organiser.

Approval and confirmation

The quote identifies the travel services included and any event arrangements handled elsewhere. Bookings are prepaid; an LPO does not provide credit. Supplier deadlines and conditions apply.
