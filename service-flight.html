Flights

Flights for the people behind your business.

Flights for annual leave, new joiners, client meetings and multi-city work trips. Compare routes, baggage and fare conditions before you choose.

What to include in your request

  • Origin and destination for each leg
  • Travel dates and flexibility
  • Traveller count, cabin and baggage
  • Preferred timings and company approver

Tell us which matters most: arrival time, fewer connections, baggage or flexibility. We’ll use that to narrow the options.

What happens next

  1. Compare journeys

    Compare the complete journey, including baggage and fare conditions.

  2. Approve the option

    Approve the exact option and amount before payment.

  3. Check your ticket

    Check the names, route and dates on the issued ticket.

Approval and confirmation

Fares and availability are checked when we prepare your quote. Bookings are prepaid; an LPO does not provide credit. Your issued ticket confirms the flight arrangement.
