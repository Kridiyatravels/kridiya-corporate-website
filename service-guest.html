Company guests

Help your company guests prepare for the UAE.

Visa assistance for clients, partners and other company guests visiting the UAE. Coordinate the application details with your nominated company contact.

What to include in your request

  • Inviting company and contact
  • Guest nationality and residence
  • Purpose and dates of visit
  • Related hotel and transfer requirements

Give us the purpose and timing of the visit first. The desk will explain the next document steps without asking you to include passport scans in this enquiry.

What happens next

  1. Confirm the visit

    Check the appropriate requirements for the planned visit.

  2. Gather the documents

    Coordinate missing information with the company contact.

  3. Follow progress

    Check application updates before finalising related travel arrangements.

Approval and confirmation

The appropriate visa requirements depend on the guest and purpose of the visit. Fees are prepaid. The issuing authority decides whether to approve the application.
