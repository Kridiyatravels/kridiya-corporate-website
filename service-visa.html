Visa support

Prepare for your next business visit.

Get help with the requirements and documents for an overseas business visit. Start with the destination, purpose of travel and intended dates.

What to include in your request

  • Destination and purpose of visit
  • Nationality and country of residence
  • Intended travel dates
  • Company sponsor or invitation details

Start with the destination, nationality and travel dates. The desk will explain which documents are needed and how to share them privately.

What happens next

  1. Check requirements

    Receive a case-specific document checklist.

  2. Prepare documents

    Provide the requested documents through the agreed private channel.

  3. Follow the application

    Follow up with the desk on preparation, submission and any further information requested.

Approval and confirmation

Visa fees are prepaid. Requirements and processing times depend on the destination and application. The issuing authority decides the outcome; payment does not guarantee approval.
