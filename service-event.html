Groups

One travel plan for your whole group.

Coordinate flights, hotel rooms and arrivals around your meeting, exhibition or company event.

What to include in your request

  • Event location, dates and organiser
  • Departure cities and estimated traveller count
  • Room types and sharing arrangements
  • Budget, approver and deadline for traveller names

An estimated group size is enough to start. Tell us when the event begins and when you expect to confirm the traveller list.

What happens next

  1. Build the group plan

    Agree the routes, rooms and arrival plan with your group organiser.

  2. Review the quote

    Review the proposed services, payment requirements and deadlines.

  3. Confirm each journey

    Check each traveller’s confirmed arrangements and travel documents.

Approval and confirmation

Group quotes set out prices, inclusions and supplier deadlines. Bookings are prepaid; an LPO does not provide credit. Individual flights, stays and transfers are confirmed separately.
