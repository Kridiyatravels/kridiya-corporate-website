Prepare a clear brief for each visit

  1. Confirm the visit details

    Share the guest’s travel dates, meeting location, purpose of visit and company host.

    Discuss visa assistance, hotel location and airport transfers before confirming the itinerary.

  3. Prepare the arrival information

    Confirm the flight number, pickup details and a reachable host contact. Share the confirmed itinerary with the guest.
