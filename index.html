A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before your
first trip.

Visit the help centre →
How do we get started?

Apply for a company account with your company details and travel needs. You can also send a travel request to the desk.

Is a travel request a confirmed booking?

No. The desk reviews your request and prepares a quote. Travel is arranged after approval and verified payment, subject to availability and supplier confirmation.

Where can we find our travel documents?

Sign in to your company workspace to follow booking updates and collect tickets and vouchers when they are released.

What if our plans change?

Contact the travel desk with your booking reference and the changes you need. Availability, charges and refund conditions depend on the relevant supplier and booking terms.
