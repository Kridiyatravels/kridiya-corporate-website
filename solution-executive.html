For executive assistants

Give your advisor the details that matter

  1. Name the travel arranger

    Confirm who can request travel, approve costs and receive the executive’s itinerary.

  2. Plan around the diary

    Share meeting locations, arrival deadlines, cabin preferences and any flexibility in the return journey.

  3. Review changes before approval

    Ask about fare flexibility, hotel cancellation terms and the cost of changing the itinerary.
