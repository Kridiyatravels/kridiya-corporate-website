Group journeys

Plan your company’s Umrah journey together.

Plan a company-sponsored Umrah journey with flights, accommodation, transport and visa assistance where applicable.

What to include in your request

  • Group organiser and number of travellers
  • Preferred travel window
  • Accommodation and transport preferences
  • Participant and package requirements

Tell us the preferred dates, group size and any assistance needs. The desk will explain the document requirements separately.

What happens next

  1. Set the group needs

    Check current requirements for the participants and travel dates.

  2. Review the package

    Review package inclusions, exclusions and supplier conditions.

  3. Prepare to travel

    Check each traveller’s details and the confirmed journey documents.

Approval and confirmation

Package availability and visa requirements are checked for your group and dates. Bookings are prepaid. Visa decisions remain with the issuing authority.
