For project coordinators

Build the travel brief around the assignment

  1. Share the location and roster

    Send the work location, team numbers, start dates and planned rotations, with a named project contact.

  2. Check the practical details

    Discuss baggage, arrival times, room sharing, length of stay and transfers between the airport and accommodation.

  3. Flag roster changes early

    Quote the booking reference and new dates when a person or schedule changes. We’ll check availability and any additional cost.
